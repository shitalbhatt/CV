Profile

An engineer's route into AI-driven diagnostics

Dr. Shital D. Bhatt is an academician and researcher with over 18 years of experience in Electronics and Communication Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Medical Image Analysis, IoT and Intelligent Systems.

Her research centres on AI-driven healthcare diagnostics, computer vision, deep learning, intelligent sensing systems, electric vehicle technologies, control systems and smart agriculture applications. She has published in peer-reviewed journals and IEEE conferences, and remains active in interdisciplinary research, curriculum development, outreach and professional leadership.
